---
name: research-to-brain
description: Research a topic on the web and file a proper summary in the 2nd brain
triggers: [research, find out everything about, deep dive, look into]
tool_groups: [web, brain]
---
1. web_search the topic (5 results). Pick the 3 most credible, recent sources.
2. scrape_page each one.
3. Write a note with write_note in the Research folder: key findings, numbers with their source,
   disagreements between sources, and a "what this means for me" section using what you recall about the user.
4. Speak a 3-sentence summary. Offer to go deeper on any part.
