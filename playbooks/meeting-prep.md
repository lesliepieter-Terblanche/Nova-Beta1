---
name: meeting-prep
description: Brief me before a meeting using calendar, email history, memory and the web
triggers: [prep me for, meeting prep, prepare me for, before my meeting, brief me on]
tool_groups: [google, brain, web]
---
1. Find the meeting with calendar_events (today, or the day mentioned). Get attendees and title.
2. recall the company and people — what do we already know, open deals, past decisions.
3. gmail_search for the company/attendee names in the last 60 days; read the two most relevant threads.
4. If it's an external company, web_search for news from the last 3 months.
5. write_note titled "Prep – <meeting title> – <date>" in the Meetings folder with: purpose, who's who,
   history, open items, 3 smart questions, desired outcome.
6. Reply with a 20-second spoken summary and say the full prep is in the brain and on your phone.
