## God's Eye View (optional, installed on request)
Nova can download and run [God's Eye View](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view) by Bilawal Sidhu
(MIT licence) into `tools/gods-eye-view`. It is not bundled with Nova. Its data sources have their own terms —
see its `DATA_SOURCES.md`. Live-data answers use adsb.lol (ODbL), USGS (public domain) and wheretheiss.at.
